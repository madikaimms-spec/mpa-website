About us

Who we are

Established in 2013, the Madikai Pravasi Association (MPA) is a community of people from Madikai Panchayath, Kasaragod, who live and work in the UAE. MPA is registered in Kerala (Reg No: KSR/124/2026).

We are dedicated to the welfare of our members and their families. We bring them together through cultural, sports and social events in the UAE, and support welfare work back home in Madikai and across Kerala.

Our focus is on strengthening individuals and promoting multicultural activities among our members and their families.

  • Strengthening individuals — standing by our members and their families.
  • Caring for home — homes, medical help and education support for families in need back in Kerala.
  • Celebrating together — Onam, Gramotsavam, music and drama that keep our traditions alive.

Back home in Kerala

Our initiatives and impact

Together, our members take part in welfare work for families back home in Madikai and Kerala.

Sneha Veedu സ്നേഹവീട്

A home for families in need. MPA builds houses and hands them over to the families who need them most. At the recent key handover ceremony for completed houses, we were joined by dignitaries including Hon. Kerala Minister Kadannappalli Ramachandran — a sign of the trust the project has earned in our community.

Palliative ambulance service

MPA supports a dedicated palliative ambulance service back home, so patients in need get comfort, dignity and timely transport to care.

Community welfare and aid

Beyond housing and medical transport, our members are involved in:

  • Donating palliative care ambulances in Kerala
  • Educational and medical help for families in need in Kerala
  • Relief support at home, including our contribution for Wayanad
  • Welfare support for our members and their families
